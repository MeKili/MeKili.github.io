Permission-aware knowledge platform

An AI-native platform for enterprise knowledge transfer. It pulls scattered sources into one model, builds a permission-aware knowledge graph with hybrid retrieval (dense, BM25 and n-hop graph fused with RRF), and answers with citations. GNNs in GraphRAG

MSc dissertation

My MSc dissertation studies when graph neural networks actually help retrieval in a GraphRAG pipeline, and how to verify it. It spans protein-complex retrieval and WebQSP knowledge-graph QA, adds explainability and a GNN-compatibility heuristic, and measures results with Hit@k, AUROC and Answer F1.

  • PyTorch
  • GNNs
  • GraphRAG
  • Neo4j
  • KGQA
